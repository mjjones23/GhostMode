# Ghost Mode AI Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 6, 2026

Ghost Mode AI ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Ghost Mode AI collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information stored on your device

Ghost Mode stores the following on your device:

- Journal entries
- Mood logs
- Streak data and healing progress
- Reminder preferences
- AI Coach conversations
- Saved coach memories, which are short preferences you choose to keep
- Account details, including your name, email address, and password credentials

If cloud sync or other online account services are added later, this policy will describe what stays on the device, what is stored online, and how long it is kept.

## How we use information

Your information powers features such as streak tracking, progress insights, daily reminders, and the AI Coach.

We use it to:

- Create and manage your account on this device
- Save your healing progress and streaks
- Store journal entries and AI Coach conversations on this device
- Provide AI Coach replies when you send a message
- Respond when you contact us for support

We do not sell your personal journal content. Journal entries stay on your device and are not sent with AI Coach requests.

## AI Coach conversations

AI Coach messages are saved on this device.

For an ordinary message, that message and recent messages from the conversation are sent to the Ghost Mode backend. When OpenAI is available, the backend asks OpenAI for a reply. If OpenAI cannot be reached, the app shows a built-in reply instead.

If the app recognizes a crisis message, it answers on this device and does not send that message, saved memories, or recovery context. A message the app does not recognize as a crisis message is sent in the ordinary way.

The coach is a self-help tool. It is not therapy, medical advice, or crisis support. AI-generated responses should not replace professional medical or mental health care.

## Saved memories

Saved memories are stored only on your device.

Sharing them with the AI Coach is optional and turned off by default. When you turn sharing on, the memories you saved are included with an ordinary coach message sent to the Ghost Mode backend. The backend includes them when it asks OpenAI for a reply. They are not included with a crisis message.

You can view and delete saved memories in Settings. Clearing the chat removes the conversation and does not delete saved memories. Deleting all local data from Settings removes saved memories together with the other data stored on this device.

## Recovery context

Sharing recovery context is separate from saved memories. It is optional and turned off by default.

When you turn it on, Ghost Mode may send only your no-contact streak day, today's mood, and recovery goal with an ordinary coach message. It does not send your journal, email, or password. Recovery context is not sent with a crisis message.

## Purchases

Ghost Mode may offer optional premium subscriptions through Apple's App Store. Payment information is processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment card information. This version makes the app's features available without a purchase.

## Data security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to help protect your information. No system is completely secure.

## How long information is kept

Journal entries, moods, streaks, reminders, AI Coach conversations, saved memories, and account details stay on your device until you delete them in the app. Deleting them removes the copy Ghost Mode stores. Ghost Mode cannot promise that a device backup no longer contains that information.

The Ghost Mode backend does not keep coach conversations, saved memories, or recovery context in a database. This version does not write coach message text into its application logs. Logs saved before that change, including logs from an earlier server version, may still contain coach messages. This policy cannot say how long the hosting provider keeps those older logs.

When the backend asks OpenAI for a reply, OpenAI processes that request. Deleting data in the app does not remove that request from OpenAI. This policy does not control how long OpenAI keeps it.

## Your choices

In Settings, you can delete one saved memory, delete all saved memories, or delete all local data. Delete account removes the on-device account, journal, moods, streak, reminders, AI Coach conversation, saved memories, and the other Ghost Mode data stored on this device.

Clearing the chat removes the conversation on this device and does not delete saved memories.

You can delete the journal entries shown in the journal.

Logging out does not delete saved data.

You can contact us with questions about this policy.

## Children's privacy

Ghost Mode is intended for people 13 and older. Before creating an account, you must confirm that you are 13 or older. That confirmation is your own statement. Ghost Mode does not ask for your birth date and does not verify your age.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes become effective when posted on this page.
