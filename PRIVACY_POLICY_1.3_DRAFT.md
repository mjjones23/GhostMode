# Ghost Mode AI Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** Set this date when the page is published. Do not publish this draft unchanged.

**Draft for version 1.3.0.** This file is local only. It does not replace https://mjjones23.github.io/ghost-mode-ai-privacy/.

Ghost Mode AI (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Ghost Mode AI collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information stored on your device

Ghost Mode stores the following on your device:

- Journal entries
- Mood logs
- Streak data and healing progress
- Reminder preferences
- AI Coach conversations
- Saved coach memories, which are short preferences you choose to keep
- Account details, including your name and email address

If cloud sync or other online account services are added later, this policy will describe what stays on the device, what is stored online, and how long it is kept.

## How we use information

Your information powers features such as streak tracking, progress insights, daily reminders, and the AI Coach.

We use it to:

- Create and manage your account on this device
- Save your healing progress and streaks
- Store journal entries and AI Coach conversations on this device
- Provide AI Coach replies when you send a message
- Keep the app reliable
- Respond to support requests
- Protect your account

We do not sell your personal journal content. Journal entries stay on your device and are not sent with AI Coach requests.

## AI Coach conversations

AI Coach messages are saved on this device. When you send a message, that message and the recent conversation are sent to the Ghost Mode backend, which asks OpenAI for a reply.

The Ghost Mode backend uses that request to produce the reply. It does not keep its own stored copy of your conversation.

The coach is a self-help tool. It is not therapy, medical advice, or crisis support. AI-generated responses should not replace professional medical or mental health care.

## Saved memories

Saved memories are stored only on your device.

Sharing them with the AI Coach is optional and turned off by default. When you turn sharing on, the memories you saved are sent to the Ghost Mode backend and OpenAI with a coach message you send.

You can view and delete saved memories in Settings. Clearing the chat removes the conversation and does not delete saved memories. Deleting all local data from Settings removes saved memories together with the other data stored on this device.

## Recovery context

Sharing recovery context is separate from saved memories. It is optional and turned off by default.

When you turn it on, Ghost Mode may send only your no-contact streak day, today’s mood, and recovery goal with a coach message. It does not send your journal, email, or password.

## Purchases

Ghost Mode AI may offer optional premium subscriptions through Apple’s App Store. Payment information is processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment card information.

## Data security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to help protect your information. No system is completely secure.

## How long information is kept

Journal entries, moods, streaks, reminders, AI Coach conversations, saved memories, and account details remain on your device until you delete them in Settings or delete the app.

A coach message, and any optional memories or recovery context you have chosen to share, is sent when you ask for a reply. The Ghost Mode backend does not keep a stored copy of that conversation. OpenAI processes the request to generate the reply.

## Your choices

You can delete saved data from Settings at any time, including one saved memory or all local data. Deleting all local data removes the on-device account, journal, moods, streak, reminders, AI Coach conversation, and saved memories.

You can also contact us to ask questions about this policy.

## Children’s privacy

Ghost Mode AI is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes become effective when posted on this page.
